# Project Design Guidelines & Persistent Rules

## Background & Visual Identity
- **Standard Global Background**: Always include `<PattachitraGrainBackground />` (from `/src/components/OdishaMotifs.tsx`) at `8%` opacity (`opacity-[0.08]`) as the general background backdrop across all new pages, views, and screens.
- **Base Canvas Color**: The standard warm neutral canvas is `#faf8f4` (`bg-[#faf8f4]`).
- **Footer Styling**: The footer uses the authentic high-resolution artwork banner (`footer_banner_art.png`) with an Apple-inspired water glass translucent card (`bg-white/35 backdrop-blur-md border-white/70`).
- **Standard Section Divider**: The authentic traditional Odisha motif artwork divider (`<OdishaDivider />` / `<SectionFlourish />` from `/src/components/OdishaMotifs.tsx`) is the standard general divider design across all sections and pages.
